# SAARTHI Access — System Limitations & Disclaimers

## 1. Decision Support, Not Legal Certification
SAARTHI Access is an assistive decision-support platform designed to help people with disabilities assess physical accessibility barriers for public journeys. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Not Legal Certification**: The verdicts and statuses provided by SAARTHI Access do not constitute official building compliance certifications, government authorizations, or legal determinations under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act or local municipal building bye-laws.

## 2. Coverage Depends on Audits and Citizen Reports
1. **The Starting State is Unknown**: When places or routes are imported into the platform, they contain **zero accessibility assumptions**. Every feature begins in an **Unknown** state until verified on-site by a human auditor or reported by independent citizens.
2. **Freshness and Decay**: All accessibility evidence is time-bound:
   - Verified auditor audits expire after 90 days.
   - Independent citizen consensus reports expire after 30 days.
   - Temporary barrier flags (e.g., broken lifts) expire after 7 days.
   Once evidence expires, the status automatically reverts to **Unknown**.
3. **No Inferred Accessibility**: The absence of reported barriers does **not** mean a facility is accessible. In SAARTHI Access, **Unknown is never accessible**.

## 3. Real-World Dynamic Hazards
Public infrastructure changes dynamically:
- Construction work, weather disruptions, temporary scaffolding, parked vehicles, and transient footpath blockages may occur between audit cycles.
- Users are encouraged to utilize the built-in **Call-Ahead Scripts** and **Helpdesk Contacts** provided on all Unknown or unverified steps before embarking on a critical journey.
