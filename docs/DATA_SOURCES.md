# Data Sources & Open Data Attribution

SAARTHI Access relies strictly on real open public data sources and community contributions.

## 1. OpenStreetMap (OSM)
- **Source**: [OpenStreetMap Foundation](https://www.openstreetmap.org/)
- **License**: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 (© OpenStreetMap contributors).
- **Attribution**: "Contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available here under the Open Database License (ODbL)."
- **Scope of Use**: Routable pedestrian network segments (footpaths, stairs, ramps, crossings, pedestrian ways), public transport stops, and baseline amenity footprints (hospitals, government offices, courts).

## 2. OpenCity / Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC)
- **Source**: [OpenCity.in](https://opencity.in/) (Pune Municipal Corporation open data repository)
- **License**: Public Domain / Government Open Data License (GODL) India.
- **Scope of Use**: Official administrative ward boundaries and municipal transport stops.

## 3. Community Audits & Citizen Reports
- **Source**: Direct on-site accessibility audits and verified citizen reports submitted via SAARTHI Access.
- **Privacy Model**: All photo uploads require explicit consent and undergo automated face and vehicle license plate blurring via OpenCV prior to persistent storage.
